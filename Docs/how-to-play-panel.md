# How To Play Panel — copy

This file IS the in-match How To Play panel (Esc menu). Edit it, then run **Tools ▸ Esc Menu ▸ Sync
How To Play Panel** in Unity to rebuild the panel from it.

Every bullet below becomes one line of the panel's body — keep each one to a single, simple
sentence. `**bold**` and `*italic*` carry over as TMP rich text, and `==word==` highlights the one
or two important words in the sentence. The H1 and this intro are ignored.

---

- ==Drag== a card from your hand onto the board to play it.
- ==Right-click== to cancel actions (playing a card, attacking, etc).
- ==Click== one of your minions, then click an enemy to attack it.
- ==Hover== over an enemy to see how much damage the attack would do before you commit.
- ==Hover== over any unit to see which spaces it can attack.
- ==Hover== over the end-turn button to see which minions will move, get blocked, or stay still.
