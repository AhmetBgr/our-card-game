# Rules & Controls — one-liners

A condensed companion to `rules-panel.md`: every bullet below is a single, simple sentence, split
into game rules (what happens) and controls (what you do to make it happen).

---

## Rules

- You win by bringing the enemy hero's Health down to 0.
- The board is a 3×3 grid: you use the bottom row, the enemy uses the top row.
- Turns switch back and forth, you go first, and you get full mana again at the start of each of your turns.
- Your max mana goes up by 1 every round, up to 10.
- You start with 3 cards, draw 1 more each turn, and can hold up to 7 cards at once.
- When you summon a minion, you pick an empty spot on your back row for it to stand.
- If that spot is taken, the minion already there gets pushed forward one space.
- A new minion can't attack or move until your next turn, unless it has Overdrive.
- At the end of your turn, every minion you control steps forward one space, if they can.
- If something is blocking a minion's path, they Clash instead of moving, and usually no damage is dealt.
- You can only attack enemies within range: melee units reach 1 space, ranged units reach 2 spaces.
- The enemy hits you back when you attack, but only if you're standing within their range too.
- A unit dies when its Health reaches 0, and its Breakdown effect happens.
- Heroes can't move, but they can hit back if attacked.
- Heroes also have passive abilities that activate on their own, like when they get attacked.
- The first time you play a card, a forged version of it gets added to your deck. 
- If your deck runs out, you get to pick from 3 forged cards instead of drawing normally.
- Picking a card this way costs your hero some Health, and it costs more each time it happens.

---

## How to Play (Controls)

- Drag a card from your hand onto the board to play it.
- Right-click to cancel actions.(Playing Card, Attacking etc)
- Click one of your minions, then click an enemy to attack it.
- Hover over an enemy to see how much damage the attack would do before you commit.
- Hover over any unit to see which spaces it can attack.
- Hover over the end-turn button to see which minions will move, get blocked, or stay still.
